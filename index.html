AI-Assisted A2 English YouTube Lesson Script Workflow

1. Problem

I create faceless English-learning videos for A2 learners on my Hello English YouTube channel. My earlier AI-assisted process depended heavily on individual prompts and manual review. This made it harder to keep lesson structure, vocabulary, grammar, and A2-level language consistent from one script to another.

2. What I Did

I built a structured Claude Project workflow for creating A2 English lesson scripts. The workflow uses four stages:

  • Gather: collect the lesson context, target vocabulary, expressions, examples, and situations.
  • Synthesize: organize the lesson objective, vocabulary, expressions, examples, and speaking practice.
  • Draft: create the complete faceless YouTube lesson script.
  • Review: check A2 level, vocabulary use, grammar, naturalness, lesson objective, speaking practice, flow, and suitability for a faceless video.

I tested the workflow with multiple real lesson topics and reviewed the output myself. The testing helped identify issues such as target vocabulary that was not reused and expressions that could be too idiomatic for A2 learners.

3. What Came of It

The workflow created a more structured and repeatable process for producing AI-assisted A2 English lesson scripts. The testing also showed where human review remained important, particularly for learner level, natural language, vocabulary reuse, and teaching quality.

Rather than relying on AI output without review, I use AI to support the scripting process while applying my teaching experience to evaluate and refine the final content.
