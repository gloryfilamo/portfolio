Hello English: AI-Assisted A2 English Lesson Script Workflow

Project: Hello English

Audience: A2 elementary English learners

Content focus: Everyday speaking confidence

1. Problem

I create faceless English-learning videos for A2 learners through my Hello English YouTube project. My earlier AI-assisted process depended heavily on individual prompts and manual review. This made it harder to keep lesson structure, vocabulary, grammar, and A2-level language consistent from one script to another.

2. What I Did

I built a structured Claude Project workflow for creating A2 English lesson scripts. The workflow includes four main stages:

  • Gather: Collect the lesson context, target vocabulary, expressions, examples, and situations.
  • Synthesize: Organize the lesson objective, vocabulary, expressions, examples, and speaking practice.
  • Draft: Create the complete faceless YouTube lesson script.
  • Review: Check A2 level, vocabulary use, grammar, naturalness, lesson objective, speaking practice, and lesson flow.

I tested the workflow with several lesson topics and examined the results myself. Testing helped identify issues such as target vocabulary that was not reused and expressions that could be too idiomatic for A2 learners.

3. What Came of It

The workflow provided a more structured and repeatable process for producing AI-assisted A2 English lesson scripts. My testing also showed where human review remained important, particularly for learner level, natural language, vocabulary reuse, and teaching quality.

I use AI to support the scripting process while applying my teaching experience and judgment to evaluate and refine the content.

A2 Lesson Script: Daily Routines

Level: A2 – Elementary

Grammar focus: Present Simple

This lesson script helps A2 learners talk about everyday routines. It includes useful vocabulary, a short story, a grammar explanation, an everyday conversation, guided speaking practice, and a quiz.

Sample excerpt:

Emma wakes up at 6:30 every morning. She gets up, brushes her teeth, and takes a shower. At 7:00, she has breakfast. Then, she goes to work.
